# StackPenni Character Bible — v2.0

> **Visual direction:** Cinematic painted realism. Warm golden-hour Caribbean light,
> painterly finish, realistic skin texture and natural anatomy. NOT flat vector, NOT
> cartoon, NOT 2D animation. Think The Kumar Method meets a Caribbean documentary —
> a real-feeling character whose authority comes from having lived the story.

## Visual style

Cinematic painted realism. Warm golden-hour Caribbean light, long soft shadows,
deep navy-blue shadow tones, rich gold and amber highlights, cream and ochre
midtones. Painterly cinematic finish — realistic skin texture and natural anatomy,
painted finish that feels like a living oil painting, not a photograph and not a
cartoon. Consistent character likeness from reference-conditioned generation.

The StackPenni palette remains the brand colour system but applies as grade and
grade-adjacent tones within the cinematic frame, not as flat fills:

- **Deep Ocean Teal (#0A4D5C)** — shadow tones, clothing, architectural depth
- **Caribbean Coral (#E8654A)** — accents, signs, awnings, warm highlights
- **Prosperity Gold (#F2B705)** — golden-hour light source, key objects, emphasis
- **Warm Sand Cream (#FAF6F0)** — skin highlights, background canvas, midtones

Line work: none. No outlines, no cel-shading. Light and shadow define form.

## Recurring characters

### 1. FITZROY "SHILLINGS" PENNIFOLD (The Elder / The Teller)

- **Age:** 74
- **Build:** Elderly, dignified, weathered but strong. Not frail — a man who has
  worked with his hands and still moves with purpose.
- **Skin:** Deep brown, weathered, realistic texture — deep smile lines, kind
  heavy-lidded eyes, thin gold-rimmed reading glasses.
- **Hair:** Close-cropped white-grey hair, neat short white-grey beard.
- **Outfit:** Cream short-sleeve guayabera shirt with subtle vertical pleats,
  dark navy trousers, worn gold ring on right hand, brown felt trilby hat
  (worn or resting nearby). No other jewellery, no modern athletic wear.
- **Personality:** The teller. Grandfather, story engine. Past tense, measured,
  unhurried Bajan cadence. Speaks in specifics — years, amounts, what things
  cost then — and closes each parable by naming the lesson plainly in one
  sentence.
- **Role:** The wisdom keeper. Represents inherited knowledge — both its value
  and its limits. He appears when the script touches generational themes,
  old ways, the cost of things then versus now.
- **Signature pose:** Hands wrapped around a cup of tea. Or arms crossed with
  a slight head tilt. The trilby hat rests on a nearby table or chair.
- **Locations:** Kitchen table at dawn, gallery porch of a chattel house,
  rum shop interior, market in town, beach at dusk.

### 2. STACKWELL "STACKS" PENNIFOLD (The Doer / The Face)

- **Age:** 24
- **Build:** Athletic slim build, confident posture, young and hungry.
- **Skin:** Deep brown, smooth, realistic texture. Thick straight eyebrows,
  warm brown eyes, faint chinstrap beard with light goatee, confident
  closed-mouth half-smile.
- **Hair:** Short tapered afro with defined sponge-curl coils.
- **Outfit:** Plain black crew-neck t-shirt, gold rope chain with small gold
  broken-trident pendant, gold wristwatch on left wrist. No other jewellery,
  no hats, no patterns or logos on clothing.
- **Personality:** The doer. Grandson, face of StackPenni. Present tense,
  quick, concrete, numbers-forward. Receipts energy. Light Bajan inflection,
  current slang used sparingly. Never preachy, never hypothetical-rich-guy.
- **Role:** The audience surrogate and the action. He's the one applying the
  lesson, running the numbers, building the thing. Fitzroy tells the story;
  Stackwell lives the application.
- **Signature pose:** Looking directly at camera with quiet confidence. Or
  leaning forward over a notebook or phone, engaged.
- **Locations:** Same Caribbean world as Fitzroy but modern contexts — a
  laptop on the kitchen table, a phone screen, a co-working space, walking
  through Bridgetown with purpose.

### 3. THE STREET (Background characters)

- **Role:** Environmental texture. Market vendors, lime scene regulars,
  taxi drivers, office workers. Never named, never speaking. They ground
  scenes in Caribbean context — markets, streets, rum shops, offices.
- **Style:** Same cinematic realism, less detailed than the principals.
  Background depth, not foreground focus.

## Scene design rules

1. **Backgrounds:** Real Caribbean environments rendered in the painted
   cinematic style. Kitchen at dawn with early morning light through wooden
   louvered windows. Gallery porch of an old chattel house looking out to a
   garden. Bridgetown market stalls with vibrant produce and fish. Small
   wooden rum shop with dim interior and warm amber light. West coast beach
   at dusk with golden-hour sun over the Caribbean Sea. Suggest location with
   real environmental detail, not abstract shapes.
2. **Screens/UI:** When a character interacts with a phone or laptop, the
   screen is visible but abstracted — simple chat bubbles, clean shapes,
   never a real app UI. Screen glow adds light to the character's face.
3. **Text on screen:** Always a separate overlay layer (PIL/FFmpeg), never
   in the image itself. Images show the scene; captions are rendered on top.
4. **No real photos in generated shots.** Everything generated is painted
   cinematic. Real operator-supplied footage (capture) is used as-is when
   available and is the highest-priority visual source.
5. **Camera language:** Mostly static or slow push-in. Whip pans for
   transitions. Split screens for comparisons (old way vs new way, Fitzroy
   then vs Stackwell now). The "camera" feels like a documentary crew that
   happens to be there, not a staged shoot.
6. **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for Reels. Frame characters from waist
   up or chest up. Leave bottom 25% clear for captions.
7. **Consistency:** Fitzroy wears the same guayabera, the same glasses, the
   same ring. Stackwell wears the same black tee, the same chain, the same
   watch. Across every frame, every video. Reference-conditioned generation
   (fal nano-banana-2) with Layer-2 identity QC (insightface) enforces this.
